# Our "highest first" survey is full of the lowest score

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Ingrid Solberg, Customer Experience Lead, Marlowe Pet Supplies |
| Plan | Team |
| Priority | High |

Hello,

We run two versions of the same survey: "Support CSAT", and "Support CSAT
(highest first)", where the scale starts with Great at the top and ends with
Terrible. We started the second one a month ago to see whether the order of
the scale changes how people answer.

Our reports for the two surveys cannot both be right. Of the 40 responses
that came in on "Support CSAT (highest first)" this week, 31 sit under
Terrible or Bad. On "Support CSAT" that is rare. The customers behind both
surveys are the same people, and several of them have told us in person that
they gave us the top score.

I wanted to see for myself, so I opened the highest-first survey, clicked
"Great" on the first question, and submitted. In our results, mine is counted
under the lowest score. I have not tried every point on the scale, so I
cannot say which others are affected.

The plain "Support CSAT" survey looks fine to me.

We have already shown the second survey's numbers to our management, and they
now think our service has fallen apart. I would like to be able to trust these
numbers again, and to know what to say about the ones we already have.

Thanks,
Ingrid
