# We removed a question and another question lost all its answers

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Anika Rao, Customer Experience Manager, Birchfield Insurance |
| Plan | Business |
| Priority | Urgent |

Hello,

I am writing because we have lost survey results, and nobody told us this
could happen.

Our "Support CSAT" survey had four questions. This week we decided that "How
easy was it to get help?" was not useful any more, so we removed it and
pressed Save survey. I expected that question to disappear and everything else
to stay exactly as it was.

The question is gone, as expected. But the last question, "Which plan are you
on?", is now empty. It still has its three options, but every one of them
shows zero answers. Before the change it had more than fifty.

Our team also noticed that in the admin view they use to look at individual
answers, some answers now sit under "Which plan are you on?" but show no plan
at all, only a number from one to five.

This was real customer feedback from the last two months. Can you tell us what
happened, and whether we can get those answers back? I would also like to know
whether removing an option from a question can do the same thing, because we
are planning to clean up several of our surveys.

Anika
