# The builder takes many seconds to open on our biggest survey

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Lukas Novak, Data Analyst, Granite Peak Outdoor |
| Plan | Enterprise |
| Priority | Medium |

Hi,

Opening our largest survey in the builder has become very slow. We simply call
it "Big survey". It has about 60 questions and has collected around 200,000
responses. When I click "Builder", I wait about 10 seconds before anything
appears. On a bad day it has been more than 15. Twice I thought the page had
frozen, reloaded it, and then waited all over again.

Our smaller surveys, with 5 to 10 questions, open straight away, so it is only
this one. When we launched it in the spring it had 40 questions and a few
thousand responses, and it opened without any delay. It seems to have got
slower as we added questions and as the responses piled up.

Nothing is broken once it has loaded. Editing and saving work, and the live
preview is fine. The network on our side is fine as well, and the other pages
in the app respond quickly. It is only that one page on that one survey.

This is not urgent, but I open this survey every week to adjust a few
questions, and the waiting adds up. We also plan to add another 20 questions
next quarter, and I am worried about what that will do.

Thanks,
Lukas
