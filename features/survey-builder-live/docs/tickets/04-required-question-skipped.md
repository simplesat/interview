# Customers can submit without answering the required first question

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Chloe Bennett, Support Lead, Cedarline Energy |
| Plan | Business |
| Priority | High |

Hi,

On our "Support CSAT" survey the first question, "How would you rate our
support today?", is marked as required. It is the number we report to the
board each quarter.

Going through the last 90 responses, I found 14 that have no answer to that
question at all. They have answers to the other questions, but the rating is
blank. That is more than one in ten, and the average we have been quoting is
built on the rest.

I asked a few of those customers what happened. Two of them told me they
simply pressed Submit and the page let them through, so I tried it myself. I
opened the survey page, answered only "How did you contact us?", left the
rating alone, and pressed Submit. I got the thank-you page, with no warning
that anything was missing.

If a question says it is required, I expect nobody to get past it without
answering. Right now I cannot say how many of our past responses are
affected, and I do not want to keep quoting numbers that I do not trust.

Please let me know what is going on.

Regards,
Chloe
