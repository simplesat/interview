# We shortened a rating scale and our old results changed

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Mei Tanaka, Product Operations Manager, Larkspur Software |
| Plan | Business |
| Priority | Urgent |

Hello,

A few weeks ago we decided that a 5-point scale was too fine for "How easy was
it to get help?", on our "Support CSAT" survey. We changed it to 3 points, and
saved it. We expected new answers to use the shorter scale and the old ones to
stay as they were.

They did not stay as they were. In the builder, results that used to say
"Terrible" now say "Bad", and answers that said "Okay" now say "Good". That is
not what those customers picked. The counts also do not add up any more.
Before the change, the numbers next to the five points added up to the number
of people who had answered. Now the numbers next to the three points add up to
far fewer. Some answers are not counted under any point at all.

Your support team kindly looked up individual answers for us in their admin
page. For many of the older rows the label column was empty, just a dash,
although the rating number was still there.

We report these results to our customers every quarter, and we have already
quoted some of them. Please tell me what happened to the old answers, whether
they can be put back the way they were, and whether it is safe for us to change
a scale again.

Thank you,
Mei
