# Our option counts moved onto other labels after we reordered them

| | |
|---|---|
| Reporter | Rafael Moreno, Head of Support Operations, Tidewater Bikes |
| Plan | Business |
| Priority | High |

Hello,

Something is wrong with our survey data and I need an answer before our
monthly review on Thursday.

Last week one of my team tidied up our "Support CSAT" survey. In "How did you
contact us?" they moved the options around so that Email sits above Phone,
because most people write to us. Then they pressed Save, and that was that.

When I opened the builder afterwards, the numbers next to the options did not
match what I remember. Email and Phone seem to have traded numbers: Email now
shows a small number and Phone a big one, when it used to be the other way
round. Nobody changed any answers, only the order of the options, so I do not
understand how that can happen. When our team looks at individual answers in
the admin view, people we know picked Phone are now listed as having picked
Email.

We use these numbers every month to decide where to put our support people.
I need to know whether the history we collected is still reliable, and
whether the same thing could have happened to other surveys we have reordered
in the past.

Thank you,
Rafael
